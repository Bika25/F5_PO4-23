Exam Preparation (Goethe / TELC)

Hero Section / Main Banner

Prepare for Your German Language Certification with Confidence Get ready for Goethe and TELC exams with structured lessons, practice tests, and expert guidance.

Duration: 8 weeks | Price: 35,000 KZT

About the Course

The German Exam Preparation course is designed for learners aiming to pass Goethe or TELC exams at A1, A2, B1, or B2 levels. This course focuses on exam strategies, practice exercises, and language skills necessary to achieve certification. You will develop listening, reading, writing, and speaking skills, familiarize yourself with exam formats, and gain confidence to succeed.

Who This Course Is For

  • Learners preparing for Goethe or TELC exams
  • Students, professionals, or travelers who need official certification
  • Anyone wanting to improve all areas of German language proficiency
  • Learners aiming to pass exams efficiently and confidently

What You Will Learn

  • Exam format and structure for Goethe and TELC tests
  • Listening comprehension strategies and practice exercises
  • Reading techniques for understanding texts quickly and accurately
  • Writing practice: letters, emails, reports, and essays
  • Speaking practice with dialogues, roleplays, and oral exam simulations
  • Grammar and vocabulary relevant to the exam levels
  • Time management and exam strategy

Course Structure

Module 1: Introduction to Exams

• Overview of Goethe and TELC exams

• Understanding scoring and assessment criteria

Module 2: Listening Skills

• Audio exercises with exam-style questions

• Techniques for catching key information

Module 3: :Reading Skills

• Comprehension strategies for short and long texts

• Practice with authentic exam materials

Module 4: Writing Skills

• Structured writing exercises for emails, letters, and essays

• Grammar and vocabulary practice for written tasks

Module 5: Speaking Skills

• Roleplay exercises and oral exam simulations

• Pronunciation, fluency, and communication strategies

Module 6: Listening and Pronunciation Practice

• Audio examples, interactive exercises, self-check quizzes
