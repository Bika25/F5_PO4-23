About the Course

The German Speaking Club is designed for learners who want to practice speaking German in a supportive, real-life environment. Through weekly live sessions, role-playing, and group discussions, you will gain confidence in speaking, improve pronunciation, and learn to use natural expressions. This course is perfect for those who struggle with speaking or want to speak fluently without hesitation. You’ll practice in a friendly, interactive, and motivating environment.

Who This Course Is For

  • Beginners who want to start speaking German naturally
  • Intermediate learners looking to improve fluency and pronunciation
  • Travelers, students, and professionals who want real-life conversation practice
  • Anyone who wants to gain confidence speaking German without fear

What You Will Learn

  • Pronunciation and speaking fluency
  • Common phrases and expressions for everyday conversations
  • Role-playing real-life situations (restaurants, travel, work, school)
  • How to speak confidently and naturally in German
  • Listening comprehension through interactive dialogue
  • Overcoming hesitation and building confidence

Course Structure

Module 1: Introduction to Speaking

• Basic techniques for speaking confidently

Module 2: Everyday Conversations

• Greetings, introductions, and small talk

Module 3: Travel and Social Situations

• Ordering food, asking for directions, social interactions

Module 4: Work and Study Conversations

• Office communication, meetings, academic discussions

Module 5: Roleplay and Real-Life Practice

• Simulated scenarios, interactive exercises, group discussions
