About the Course

The Reading Skills Course is designed for learners who want to develop comprehension and fluency in reading German. Through structured lessons, practical exercises, and carefully selected texts, you will learn to read faster, understand the meaning, and extract key information. This course helps learners gain confidence in reading German newspapers, books, emails, and other real-life texts while improving vocabulary and grammar in context.

Who This Course Is For

  • Beginners who want to improve reading comprehension
  • Intermediate learners who want to read longer texts fluently
  • Students preparing for exams or academic purposes
  • Professionals who want to read German documents, emails, and articles effectively

What You Will Learn

  • Techniques for understanding and analyzing texts
  • Strategies to guess meaning from context
  • Expanding vocabulary while reading
  • Identifying key ideas, themes, and details
  • Understanding grammar and sentence structure in context
  • Practice with real-life German texts

Course Structure

Module 1:Introduction to Reading German

•Basic reading techniques, tips, and strategies

Module 2: Everyday Texts

• Emails, messages, advertisements, menus, and notices

Module 3:Articles and Stories

• Short stories, news articles, blogs, and cultural texts

Module 4: Academic and Professional Texts

• Work emails, instructions, reports, and formal letters

Module 5: Vocabulary Building Through Reading

• Learn new words in context and retain them effectively
