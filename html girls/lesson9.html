About the Course

The German B1: Intermediate course is designed for learners who have completed A2 or have basic-intermediate knowledge of German. You will improve fluency, understand more complex texts, and participate in conversations confidently. This course focuses on practical language skills for daily life, work, travel, and study, while expanding grammar, vocabulary, and cultural understanding.
