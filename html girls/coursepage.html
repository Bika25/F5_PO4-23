Course Overview

This course is designed to help you build strong German language skills step by step. You will learn essential vocabulary, grammar structures, and practical communication skills needed for everyday situations.

What you will learn

  • Introductions and basic conversations
  • German alphabet and pronunciation
  • Grammar basics (articles, cases, verbs)
  • Building simple sentences
  • Daily-life vocabulary

Who is this course for?

  • Complete beginners
  • Students planning to study in Germany
  • Anyone interested in learning German from scratch

Syllabus

Week 1 – Basics
Alphabet, pronunciation, greetings, numbers.

Week 2 – Grammar Essentials
Articles, verb conjugation, sentence structure.

Week 3 – Daily Vocabulary
Food, shopping, hobbies, family.

Week 4 – Dialogues
Introducing yourself, simple conversations.

Week 5 – Practice
Listening tasks, real-life exercises.

Week 6 – Final Test
Speaking & writing tasks, certificate.
