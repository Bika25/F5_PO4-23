Learn German Naturally by Watching Movies
Improve your listening, comprehension, and vocabulary with your favorite films.

About the Course

German Through Movies” is an engaging course designed for learners who want to learn German in a fun and natural way. By watching carefully selected films and scenes, you will understand real-life conversations, slang, and cultural references. This course helps learners improve listening skills, expand vocabulary, and practice speaking, all while enjoying cinema. Perfect for both beginners and intermediate students who want to learn through context..

Who This Course Is For

  • Beginners and intermediate learners who want to improve listening and comprehension
  • Movie lovers who want to learn German through real conversations
  • Students preparing for exams or looking to expand practical vocabulary
  • Anyone who wants a fun and immersive way to learn German

What You Will Learn

  • Understand spoken German in natural dialogue and different accents
  • Learn common phrases, idioms, and expressions used in films
  • Expand vocabulary in context and remember it better
  • Improve pronunciation and listening comprehension
  • Analyze scenes to understand grammar and sentence structure

Course Structure

Module 1: Introduction to Film-Based Learning

• How to use movies for language learning

• Tips for listening and repeating naturallyp

Module 2: Everyday Conversations in Movies

• Greetings, small talk, and casual dialogue

Module 3: Travel and Social Situations

• Scenes in restaurants, cafes, airports, and public places

Module 4: Work and School Scenes

• Conversations at the office, school, or university

Module 5: Cultural Expressions and Idioms

• Common phrases and slang used in films
