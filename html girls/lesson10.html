About the Course

The German Conversation Course is designed for learners who want to develop speaking skills and engage in natural conversations. Through interactive lessons, dialogues, and role-playing, you will practice speaking confidently in various situations. This course is perfect for learners who struggle with fluency or want to express themselves clearly in social, professional, or travel contexts.

Who This Course Is For

  • Beginners and intermediate learners wanting to improve speaking skills
  • Students, professionals, and travelers who need practical conversation practice
  • Anyone aiming to speak German naturally and confidently
  • Learners preparing for B1/B2 level exams or real-life communication

What You Will Learn

  • Engage in everyday conversations and small talk
  • Practice phrases and vocabulary for social, travel, and work situations
  • Roleplay real-life scenarios such as restaurants, travel, office, and school
  • Improve pronunciation, intonation, and fluency
  • Gain confidence speaking spontaneously in German
  • Understand cultural expressions and communication styles

Course Structure

Module 1: Basics of Conversation

• Greetings, introductions, and polite expressions

Module 2: Everyday Situations

• Shopping, dining, traveling, and socializing

Module 3:Work and Study Conversations

• Office dialogue, meetings, presentations, and classroom discussions

Module 4: Cultural Expressions

• Sports, music, movies, social interactions

Module 5: Roleplay and Practical Practice

• Simulated scenarios, interactive exercises, group discussions
