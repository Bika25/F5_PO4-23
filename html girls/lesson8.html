Take Your German Skills to the Next Level Build on your basics and start communicating more confidently in German.

About the Course

The German A2: Elementary course is designed for learners who already have a basic understanding of German (A1 level) and want to develop stronger speaking, listening, reading, and writing skills. You will learn more complex grammar, expand vocabulary, and practice real-life dialogues, preparing you for everyday situations, travel, work, or further study.

Who This Course Is For

  • Learners who completed A1 or have basic German knowledge
  • Travelers, students, or professionals wanting practical communication skills
  • Anyone who wants to speak, understand, and write in German more confidently
  • Learners preparing for the A2 German exam

What You Will Learn

  • Expanded vocabulary for travel, work, hobbies, and social life
  • More advanced grammar: past tense, modal verbs, word order, and sentence structure
  • Real-life conversations in social, travel, and professional contexts
  • Listening comprehension with longer dialogues and audio materials
  • Writing short texts, emails, and simple descriptions
  • Understanding cultural contexts in German-speaking countries

Course Structure

Module 1: Grammar Review & Expansion

• Revisiting A1 basics, introducing past tense and modal verbs

Module 2: Everyday Life Vocabulary

•Food, shopping, health, hobbies, and leisure activities

Module 3: Travel & Directions

•Booking hotels, asking for directions, navigating public transport

Module 4: Work & Study Conversations

•Office, school, meetings, and classroom dialogues
