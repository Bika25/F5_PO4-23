About the Course

The German A1: Beginner course is designed for learners who are starting their journey in German. You will learn the fundamentals of grammar, build essential vocabulary, and practice simple conversations for everyday life. This course will help you communicate in basic situations, understand simple written and spoken German, and prepare for the A1 German language exam if desired.

Who This Course Is For

  • Absolute beginners with little or no knowledge of German
  • Travelers, students, and professionals wanting to learn practical German
  • Anyone who wants to build a solid foundation in grammar, vocabulary, and basic conversations
  • Learners aiming to progress to A2 and higher levels

What You Will Learn

  • Basic German grammar: articles, verbs, adjectives, and sentence structure
  • Everyday vocabulary for home, travel, shopping, and social interactions
  • Simple dialogues and conversations for greetings, introductions, and daily life
  • Listening comprehension of basic spoken German
  • Writing simple texts such as messages, emails, or short descriptions
  • Cultural insights and etiquette in German-speaking countries

Course Structure

Module 1:Introduction to German

• Alphabet, pronunciation, and basic greetings

Module 2: Everyday Vocabulary

• Family, home, shopping, food, and numbers

Module 3: Grammar Basics

•Articles, pronouns, verbs (present tense), and sentence structure

Module 4: Simple Conversations

• Introducing yourself, asking questions, ordering food, traveling
