German for Travel

Hero Section / Main Banner

Learn Essential German for Traveling Abroad Master key phrases, vocabulary, and conversations for a smooth travel experience in Germany and other German-speaking countries.

Duration: 3 weeks | Price: 18,500 KZT

About the Course

German for Travel” is designed for travelers who want to communicate confidently in German during trips. This course focuses on practical, real-life phrases and vocabulary needed for airports, hotels, restaurants, shopping, and sightseeing. With interactive lessons, audio exercises, and role-play practice, you will be able to handle common travel situations smoothly and confidently.

Who This Course Is For

  • Beginners planning to travel to Germany, Austria, or Switzerland
  • Travelers who want to communicate effectively in everyday situations
  • Students and professionals preparing for short trips or study abroad
  • Anyone who wants to feel confident using German while traveling

What You Will Learn

  • Essential travel phrases for greetings, directions, and transportation
  • Ordering food and drinks in restaurants and cafes
  • Booking hotels, asking for services, and handling check-ins
  • Shopping and negotiating prices
  • Asking for help, directions, and travel information
  • Cultural tips and etiquette for German-speaking countries

Course Structure

Module 1: Getting Started

• Greetings, introductions, and polite expressions

Module 2: Transportation and Directions

• Airports, trains, buses, taxis, and asking for directions

Module 3: Accommodation

• Booking hotels, checking in/out, and asking for service

Module 4: Food and Dining

•Ordering in restaurants, cafes, and food markets

Module 5:Shopping and Sightseeing

• Asking for prices, buying tickets, sightseeing vocabulary
